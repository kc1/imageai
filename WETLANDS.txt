How to Find Parcel Endpoints for Your Target Area
Because there is no single free nationwide public parcel API provided by the US federal government, you have two options depending on your scope:

Free Public County ArcGIS Endpoint (Single State or County focus):

Search Google for: "[County Name] [State] GIS parcel MapServer rest services"

Example: "Wake County NC GIS parcel MapServer rest services"

Look for a URL ending in /MapServer/0 or /FeatureServer/0 that contains layers named Parcels, Tax Lots, or Property Lines.

Nationwide Commercial APIs (Multi-state / Production applications):

Regrid API: The industry standard for US parcel data. Provides nationwide API endpoints for GeoJSON boundaries and parcel attributes.

Geocodio: Provides parcel boundary geometries alongside reverse geocoding for US addresses
